The Problem

You've read the same paragraph three times. It still hasn't stuck.

It's not you. Walls of text make your brain do double duty: translate the words into a mental picture, then actually learn the idea. By page 30, you're not studying anymore. You're just surviving.

CogniFlow does the translating for you. It's an adaptive visual reviewer that pulls out the core ideas, throws away the index clutter, and hands you something you can actually review.

What You Get
01 • Skip the boilerplate, keep the lesson Upload a whole textbook or paste a long excerpt, with no size limits. CogniFlow ignores page numbers, copyright pages, and index lists, and goes straight to the chapter you name.
02 • Show, don't tell Every reading becomes a presentation deck: a clear visual schematic on the left, distilled bullet takeaways on the right. Flip through it like a deck, or switch to a one-page notes sheet.
